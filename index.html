Ask HR

RAG + GPT

Ask a question about company policy. The assistant always searches the policy documents first, answers only from them, and names the source. It remembers the last 10 messages of the conversation. If the policies don't cover the question, it suggests contacting HR or IT.

Second project

Pharmacy requests on WhatsApp

Dawaa Pharmacy automation. Customers send a medicine name or a photo of a prescription on WhatsApp. The system reads it, checks the catalog, reserves the stock, and asks a pharmacist on Telegram whenever a human check is needed.

WhatsApp and Telegram triggers

Requests and reservations

Dawaa 1 · Requests & Reservations

A customer sends a medicine name or a photo of a prescription. AI reads the request and the workflow matches it to the catalog. Medicines that are in stock are reserved straight away. Controlled or low-stock medicines go to a pharmacist on Telegram, who approves or rejects with one tap.

Message the pharmacy on WhatsApp

Preview with sample data. WhatsApp and Telegram are simulated here. Send a request on the phone, then use the pharmacist's buttons.

Runs every 15 minutes and daily

Expiry and stock alerts

Dawaa 2 · Maintenance

Two schedules keep reservations and stock honest. Every 15 minutes, holds past their time are cancelled, the customer is told on WhatsApp, and the stock goes back to the catalog. Every day at 22:00, medicines at or below their reorder level are sent to the team on Telegram and to purchasing by email.

Preview with sample data. These run on a timer in real life, so use the buttons on the phone to run them now.

Runs on error

Pharmacy error handler

Dawaa 2 · Error handling

When a pharmacy workflow fails, the error is classified (credentials, rate limit, network, Google Sheets, messaging or code), logged to an Errors sheet, and sent to the team on Telegram. Critical errors, such as credential or data problems, also trigger an email.

Preview with sample data. Trigger an error on the phone to see how each type is handled.
